Boundaries for Imagination

Outdoor Portraits

Draw or paint nature inspired art. Example: Sketch a tree or flower in your garden.

Book Review Rally

Share opinions on books. Example: “I liked this book because the main character was brave.”

Write in Sand or on Sidewalk

Encourage outdoor writing practice. Example: Use chalk to write spelling words or create short stories.

Mood Tracker

Use colors or stickers to track emotions. Example: Yellow = happy, blue = calm, red = excited.

You can change the rules.

Rules can help us play, but they can also change when we discover something new.

Imagination Trail

Create a pretend play path and use it to create a story. Example: pretend stops on the path are a jungle, the moon, or a candy kingdom.

Get Cooking!

Get busy in the kitchen to make a tasty treat! Example: Make pizza together, choosing toppings and reading the recipe.

Plant Something

Grow a fruit, vegetable, or flower from scratch. Example: Plant seeds and track their growth each week.

DIY Crafts

Hands on projects to encourage creativity. Example: Make paper lanterns or friendship bracelets.

Spelling Obstacle Course

Combine physical activity with spelling practice. Example: Jump over pillows while spelling “elephant.”

Write a Song

Explore music and words together. Example: Create a song about a day at the park using simple rhymes and a catchy tune.

Make Your Own Comic Book

Combine illustrations with storytelling. Example: Draw a superhero saving a cat from a tree and write speech bubbles for dialogue.

Visit a Museum

Inspire creativity by exploring exhibitions. Example: Explore a science exhibit and discuss discoveries.

Letters to Characters.

Write to favorite book or movie characters. Example: “Dear Harry Potter, what’s your favorite spell?”

Story Quest.

Use story prompts to create your own adventure. Example: Start with “You discover a secret door in your school…”

Story Cards

Cards with characters, settings, or actions inspire stories. Example: Draw a card that says “A dragon in the library” and make up a story around it.

Story Map

Plan characters, plot, and settings with printable templates. Example: Map out the journey of a lost kitten finding its way home.

Picture Writing Prompts

Use images to spark storytelling and descriptive writing. Example: Show an image of a beach and ask your child to create a story about finding hidden treasure in the sand.

Unscramble Sentences

Rearrange words to form correct sentences. Example: “dog / The / barked / loudly” → “The dog barked loudly.”

Write to a Pen Pal

Exchange letters or write to their future self. Example: “Dear Future Me, what’s your favorite memory of this year?”
