Boundaries for Imagination

RULE 01

Don't throw things.

We can be playful and energetic while keeping ourselves and others safe.

RULE 02

Everyone gets a turn.

Imagination can be shared. Give everyone a chance to contribute.

RULE 03

You can make a mess.

Mess can be part of making. We can explore first and clean up afterward.

RULE 04

Listen to each other.

Everyone has ideas. Listening gives imagination room to grow together.

RULE 05

You can change the rules.

Rules can help us play, but they can also change when we discover something new.

RULE 06

Ask before taking.

Other people's objects and ideas belong to them too.

RULE 07

Parents can play too.

Sometimes the best way to understand a child's imagination is to enter it.

RULE 08

Make your own rule.

What rule would make your imagination feel safe, free, and fun?

RULE 09

Ask a question.

Questions can open up new ways of seeing and imagining.

RULE 10

Try something new.

There is no single right way to make, play, or imagine.

RULE 11

Use what you have.

Everyday objects can become something unexpected.

RULE 12

Look at it differently.

A small change in perspective can create a completely new idea.

RULE 13

Take your time.

Imagination does not always need to happen quickly.

RULE 14

Make mistakes.

Mistakes can become part of discovering something new.

RULE 15

Change your mind.

An idea can grow when you allow yourself to change direction.

RULE 16

What could this become?

Let your imagination decide what comes next.
